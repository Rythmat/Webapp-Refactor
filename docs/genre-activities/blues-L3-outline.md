# Blues L3 “Jazz Blues” — activity outline (for Peter’s review)

Built from Peter’s ICP Studio blues material (BLUES-LEVELS-FOR-MUSIC-ATLAS). Keys: F, B♭; the tunes in C, F, B♭.
**doc** = the doc’s notes exactly; **adapted** = the doc’s material with the change noted;
**draft** = built from Peter’s rules where ICP has no material yet; **added** = a scale step the Music Atlas format puts before phrases; **moved** = the doc’s material in another key. Authored in `blues_v2.ts`.

- Everywhere: Jazz Swing (kick on 1); eighths swung as ICP plays lines (64% at 120).
- Peter’s jazz blues form in F: F7 | F7 | F7 | Cm7 F7 | B♭7 | B♭7 | F7 | F7 D7♭9 | Gm7 | C7 | F7 D7♭9 | Gm7 C7. Two chords in a bar are chordsPerBar: 2.
- Chord labels name the extensions the voicings play (F13, Cm9, G13♭9), so the app can find each chord in the notes.
- The tunes are comped over their changes only: the heads are copyrighted, and students read them from their own books.
