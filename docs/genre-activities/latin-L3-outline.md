# Latin L3 "Bossa · Tango · Afro-Cuban" — activity outline (for Aaron's sign-off)

Built from Latin Band 1's L3 variations, all **E minor over the same
Em – Am – B7 – Em**, colored per style. **take** = the band's recorded part
(simplified where noted); **draft** = Claude's proposal, needs Aaron's OK.
Authored in `latin_v2.ts` (40 steps).

The Afro-Cuban style has two variations: 2-3 plays by default; A6.3, B3.3
and D3.2 (steps 16, 25, 40) play the 3-2 one (`lessonPairings/latin-l3-afro-cuban.json`).
The 2-3 groove plays **rumba** clave (the three side ends on the "and" of 4),
the 3-2 groove **son** clave; the clave steps teach exactly those.

Samba: no samba variation in the band yet, so the style teaches bossa only.
The console has a Samba groove and Samba / Partido Alto bass lines to build one.
