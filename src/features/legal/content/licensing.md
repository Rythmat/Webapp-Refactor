**1.1. Individual License (Artist Account)**

Grants access to Music Atlas and/or Music Prism for a single user. Non-transferable, for personal use only. May not be shared, sublicensed, or used in classrooms or commercial settings.

**1.2. Studio License (Private Teachers & Studios)**

Allows instructors to use Music Atlas with up to 25 student accounts. Includes lesson-tracking tools and access to printable or digital resources. Not valid for district-level or public school use.

**1.3. Classroom License (Schools & Institutions)**

Designed for public and private school classrooms. Licenses are issued per teacher (up to 10 classrooms).

**1.4. District License**

Allows use across multiple classrooms and schools. Includes administrative dashboards, educator training, and priority support. Custom pricing and implementation available through contract.

**1.5. Music Prism Plugin License**

Available as individual, academic, or commercial licenses. License terms vary depending on use (personal, educational, or professional production). Commercial use (e.g., music released commercially or used in client projects) requires a commercial upgrade.
