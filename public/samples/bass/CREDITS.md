# Bass samples

Both by **Karoryfer Samples**, released under **CC0 1.0** — no attribution
required, credited anyway. Files are renamed to scientific pitch, trimmed
to the attack, cut to about 2.8 seconds and gain-matched note to note, with
per-file peak control (nothing above -2.5 dBFS). One dynamic layer and one
round robin per note. `gainDb` in INSTRUMENTS (src/curriculum/engine/bassLines/bassLine.ts) lines
the two up with each other.

## upright/
**Meatbass**: a 1958 Otto Rubner double bass with Thomastik-Infeld
Spirocore strings, played and mapped by Drogomir Smolken. Pizzicato,
third of four velocity layers. Library names are standard pitch.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.meatbass

## electric/
**Black And Blue Basses**, the "babyblue" bass: regular finger plucks,
forte layer. Sampled chromatically B0 to D4, so nothing is pitch-shifted.
Library names its octaves one high; renamed here.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses
