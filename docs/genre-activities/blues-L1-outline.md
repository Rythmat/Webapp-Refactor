# Blues L1 “Shuffle Blues” — activity outline (for Peter’s review)

Built from Peter’s ICP Studio blues material (BLUES-LEVELS-FOR-MUSIC-ATLAS). Keys: C, G.
**doc** = the doc’s notes exactly; **adapted** = the doc’s material with the change noted;
**draft** = built from Peter’s rules where ICP has no material yet; **added** = a scale step the Music Atlas format puts before phrases; **moved** = the doc’s material in another key. Authored in `blues_v2.ts`.

- Everywhere: the Blues Shuffle; eighths written straight and played on the triplets, as ICP plays the piano (swing 67).
- Grace notes written on the beat, the note after them a 32nd late.
- Blues Band 1 plays under the play-alongs, its comping written from the doc’s voicings (Blues Pianist 1), one variation per chart (lessonPairings/blues-l1-shuffle.json).
- Both 8-bar forms are the doc’s proposals (ICP has none): A, I–V–IV–IV–I–V–I–V (“Key to the Highway”); B, I–I–IV–IV–I–V–I–V.
