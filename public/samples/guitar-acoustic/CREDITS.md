# Acoustic guitar samples

From **tonejs-instruments** by Nicholas Brosowsky
(https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), whose samples are
released under **CC BY 3.0** — attribution required. Its
`sample-source-info.txt` gives the acoustic guitar's source as the
University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios
(http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/).

Steel-string acoustic, one note per file, D2 to D5, named by scientific
pitch with "s" for a sharp (`Fs3.mp3` is F♯3). The Studio's
`GUITAR_ACOUSTIC_CONFIG` (src/daw/instruments/sampleConfigs.ts) maps them;
Tone.js's Sampler pitch-shifts between and beyond them.
