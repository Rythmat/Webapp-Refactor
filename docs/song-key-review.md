# Song key review

The key audit of all 640 songs (September 2026). Each song was checked against published sheet music (Musicnotes, Hal Leonard), Hooktheory and other transcriptions. The label has to match the key the chart's chords are written in.

**Already fixed (105 songs):** high-confidence wrong labels, and 13 labels whose sharp or flat spelling didn't match the chart (D♭ minor → C♯ minor). The full list is at the end.

**Held for you:** everything below. Write the key in "Your answer". Leave it blank to accept the suggestion, or write "keep" to keep the current label.

## 7. Unmarked modulations found by the ♯-degree scan (18)

Every chart was scanned for chords whose hybrid degree reads `♯1`, `♯2`, `♯5` or `♯6`.
Those readings mean the chord is being numbered against the wrong tonic — either the
section has modulated and nothing marks it, or the chart is spelled in the wrong
enharmonic. `♯4` is excluded (a real Lydian/blues degree), and so is any diminished
chord (a passing `F♯dim7` between F and Gmin7 genuinely _is_ `♯1`).

Fourteen charts turned out to be pure spelling slips, fixable by respelling one chord
(B→C♭, E→F♭). Three were applied, the ones where the artifact hid a structural degree:
**Tears Of A Clown** and **Beyond** (B→C♭, `♯6`→`♭7`) and **Tell Me Something Good**
(B→C♭, `♯2`→`♭3`). The other eleven are all `♭6` cases, left as written because `B` and
`E` read more easily than `C♭` and `F♭`: Ain't Nobody, Off The Wall, Virtual Insanity,
Rock With You, Sledgehammer, Superstition, Hit The Road Jack, (They Long To Be) Close To
You, Umbrella, Burn This Disco Out, Golden.
