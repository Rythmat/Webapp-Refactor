# Latin L2 "Merengue · Bachata · Cha-cha-chá · Bolero" — activity outline (for Aaron's sign-off)

Built from Latin Band 1's L2 variations. Every take is in **E minor over
Em – Am – B7 – Em**, one chord a bar, so the lessons are too: the recorded
parts play as written under each step and line up bar for bar. **take** = the
band's recorded part (simplified where noted); **draft** = Claude's proposal,
needs Aaron's OK. Authored in `latin_v2.ts` (44 steps).

| Style       | styleRef | Tempo | Drums (groove) | Keys take                   | Bass take         | Feel                              |
| ----------- | -------- | ----- | -------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Merengue    | l2a      | 120   | Merengue 3-2   | Merengue Montuno (take 9)   | Merengue (take 1) | RH on the beats, LH on the "and"s |
| Bachata     | l2b      | 148   | Bachata 1      | Bachata Montuno (arpeggios) | Bachata 1-5-1     | 3-note roll across 8 eighths      |
| Cha-cha-chá | l2c      | 130   | Cha Cha Cha    | Cha-cha-chá take 12         | Cha Cha Bassline  | "4 & 1" — cha-cha-chá             |
| Bolero      | l2d      | 90    | Latin - Bolero | Bolero take 13 (+ guitar 6) | Bolero            | chords on every "and", bass 3+3+2 |

Tempos are the band's: Bachata's and Cha-cha's variations set them; Merengue
and Bolero set none (the band's 90 plays in the Band editor), so Merengue takes
its groove's 120 and Bolero stays at 90. In-time practice steps name a slower
tempo of their own.

**Level theme — the three minor scales:** natural minor (Merengue), harmonic
minor (L1 Cumbia, used again over B7 here), melodic minor (Bolero).
