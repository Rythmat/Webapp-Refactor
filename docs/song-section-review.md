# Song section review

Every one of the 640 charts now carries real section names (Intro, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Interlude, Tag, Outro) instead of the parser’s “Section A/B/C”, and a test keeps it that way.

**How they were named.** The charts were cut into 4-bar fragments by the PDF parser, so naming them meant recovering the form first: fragments merge into real sections, and a section repeated back to back is written once between repeat signs. Bars were only regrouped — never moved, dropped or altered.

- **211 songs were read individually** (by research agents or by me), using the recording plus the chord evidence.
- **429 songs were named by structure alone** — repetition, length and position: the most repeated material is the Verse, the next most the Chorus, a short opener the Intro, a short closer the Outro, the longest one-off section in the middle the Bridge. That is right for most popular songs and wrong for some; they are listed at the end.
